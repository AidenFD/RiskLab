Dependencies retain their own licensing. The source package installs them
from their distributions; it does not change their licenses.

Runtime: NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), pyqtgraph (MIT), Colorama (BSD-3-Clause), and
PySide6/Shiboken/Qt (their LGPL/GPL/commercial distribution terms).
Development: pytest, pytest-qt, PyInstaller and its hooks retain their
respective distribution licenses and applicable bootloader exceptions.

Inspect the dependency distributions' LICENSE/NOTICE files for complete terms
when distributing a built executable. Build on the target platform and preserve
relevant bundled third-party notices. BacktestLab and OrderBookLab are product
references, not bundled source or runtime dependencies.
